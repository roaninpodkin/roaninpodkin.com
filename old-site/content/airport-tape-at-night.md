**On this tape:** Roanin Podkin and Dennis Yu.

  

14er in the morning. Airport tape at night. I hiked Mt Bierstadt with [Jason Amato](https://jasongamato.com/) and the operators from his lab, then [Dennis Yu](https://dennisyu.com/) sat me down at Denver International Airport until I had to go for a 7:20 flight.

The full Studio Sound cut is 22 minutes 15 seconds. Phone, no lav. Suitcase out of frame. This is the sister page to [episode one](https://roaninpodkin.com/days-with-roanin-episode-1/).

![Roanin Podkin and Dennis Yu at Denver International Airport](../media/2026/08/roanin-airport-two-shot.jpg)

DEN, 29 August 2026. The last line on the recording is my flight.

## What is on this tape

The hike as trust, not as a vendor outing. SDR as a title that reads as hassle. Jason inviting me on the mountain. Seven ICP demos at that workshop — four verbal, one still working. Change the public title. Owners buy trust. Chop yesterday’s podcast. Look back in 90 days.

The other stories on this site unpack those pieces. This page is the whole chair.
