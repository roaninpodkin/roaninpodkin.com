**On this tape:** Roanin Podkin and Dennis Yu.

  

Dennis asked how the workshop went. There were fewer than a dozen home-service companies. I told him the number on tape: we got seven to actually book. We had four on a verbal. One other I am still working. Everything that was ICP — companies I believe we provide value to. That excludes companies I do not think are in the ICP.

That is that room, that week.

![Roanin Podkin and Dennis Yu at Denver International Airport after the workshop](../media/2026/08/roanin-airport-two-shot.jpg)

Denver International Airport, 29 August 2026. Phone, no lav. The number on the tape is the workshop.

## Why he thinks it happened

He argued it was not great sales skills. It was trust in a trusted environment. Low-risk next step because they had already decided I was a good dude. Then he dated a prediction: that I will be the number-one sales-driving talent at Avoca. He said it before it is true, on purpose, so the tape exists. Look back in 90 days from 29 August 2026. A prediction is not a result. I am not going to print it as one.

## The number on this page

The workshop count I said with my own mouth is the number that belongs here. Dennis’s on-tape aside about a raise is his line, not a figure I can verify.
