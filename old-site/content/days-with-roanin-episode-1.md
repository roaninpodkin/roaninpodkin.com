**On this tape:** Roanin Podkin and Dennis Yu.

  

I called it episode one. The working title on the tape is _Days with Roanin_. I sat down with [Dennis Yu](https://dennisyu.com/) in Broomfield on 28 August 2026 and we picked up a conversation we had already started: how you choose people, how perceived influence becomes real, and how an SDR at [Avoca](https://www.avoca.ai/) uses that loop without turning into a tools pitch.

The recording is on the Local Service Spotlight Descript drive as an unlisted share until a YouTube handle exists. This page is the written hub. Quotes are from the Studio Sound cut (54 minutes 11 seconds). I said my own name wrong once on tape. The spelling is Roanin.

![Roanin Podkin and Dennis Yu at the table for episode one](../media/2026/08/roanin-friday-two-shot.jpg)

Broomfield, 28 August 2026. I am in the Avoca pullover. Dennis is in the Dunk hoodie. Phone on the table.

## Why we hit record

I asked him, as somebody who has a lot of freedom, what he looks for when he chooses people to work with. His first filter was not a spreadsheet. Find someone who has already done the thing you want. Then, simpler than that: who do you like spending time with. Friendship first. The technical calculation comes after, if it comes at all.

He used the heart-surgeon test. You cannot audit the surgery. You use reviews, friends, patients. For a contractor, the reviews are perceived influence. The job on your HVAC system is the real thing. They are two sides of the same coin. You cannot have one without the other.

## Jason put the conference together

He pulled me out of the esoteric version. We were in that room because [Jason Amato](https://jasongamato.com/) put the conference together. That is perceived influence. A couple of days later it is actual influence, because I ate the food. Potential versus kinetic. Most people leave it as potential.

## If money did not matter

He asked what I would do if I were not paying rent. I said I would hang out with interesting people and go down the list of things I have not done yet — Kilimanjaro, a 14er. We were already climbing Mt Bierstadt the next morning. Avoca brought me to meet specific people. I also wanted to be there. That is not a trade-off. That is still doing the SDR job and the life I want in the same week.

## Is the mission visible?

I do not post much on LinkedIn. Person to person, I think the mission is obvious. He asked if it is visible publicly. This recording is the start of multiplying that. He asked if I had a personal brand site. I said no. The site is the byproduct, not the goal.

## Close

I closed it as podcast number one. He said we will see each other on the mountain. We did.
