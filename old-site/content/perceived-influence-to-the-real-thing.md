**On this tape:** Roanin Podkin and Dennis Yu.

  

I asked [Dennis Yu](https://dennisyu.com/) when perceived influence becomes influence. His answer on episode one: it is always perceived, until you sit down and eat the food.

CNN is perceived. The contractor who actually fixes the unit is real. The five-star restaurant you have never entered is still a rumor. Then you eat. Then your firsthand experience becomes perceived influence for the next person.

## The room we were actually in

He told me my IQ was too high for the question. We were hanging out because [Jason Amato](https://jasongamato.com/) put this conference together. Great conference. People said great things about it. That is the simple version of building perceived influence: be in the room the operators already trust, then do the work so the next room is earned.

Potential versus kinetic. Most people collect the invitation and leave it as potential. I stayed for the mountain the next morning. That is the kinetic half.

## The site followed the tape

I did not have a site when we hit record. I said so on tape. The site is a byproduct of recording the conversation and putting it where a stranger can inspect it.

If you want the method in Dennis’s voice from the same week, he published [The Magic Berry Principle](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gp6HrlC5VRw) on the Local Service Spotlight channel. That video is his. This article is mine, from the hour we actually sat.

![Roanin Podkin and Dennis Yu during episode one](../media/2026/08/roanin-friday-two-shot.jpg)

The conversation is the proof. The logo on my pullover is Avoca. The work is still to be known as a person, not as a demo-booker.
