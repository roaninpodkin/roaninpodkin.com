# About Roanin Podkin

I am a sales development representative at [Avoca](https://www.avoca.ai/). Santa Barbara office. Joined July 2026. UCSB Economics. Before Avoca I ran an AI consulting practice. The public new-hire post is 17 July 2026. Spelling is **Roanin**, not Ronan.

Avoca is the AI front office for home-service businesses — calls, texts, and chat that book into the CRM. My seat is sales development. In a room of plumbers and HVAC owners that title reads as vendor. The work is to be in the room as a person they already trust.
