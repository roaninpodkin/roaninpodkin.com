I came back from Asia in the middle of the AI boom, and I saw it as the biggest opportunity out there.

I started watching everything I could find. An hour a day minimum, most days four or five. Then I started testing: asking the models to do real tasks, seeing how accurate they were, building agents, automating easy work.

I started telling other people about it. Then I started doing it for them. I got a business license as an AI consultant.
