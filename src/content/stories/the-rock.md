My mom tells this story more than I do.

I was about three years old, in Vacaville. I tried to step off a rock and fell. It made me so frustrated that I climbed back on and stepped off again. And again. Not until I got it right once, but until I couldn't get it wrong.

I don't remember it. But I recognize it. That's been the theme of my whole life: the will to work at something until it's done.
