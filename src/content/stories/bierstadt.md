On August 28, 2026, I sat down with [Dennis Yu](https://dennisyu.com/) in Broomfield, Colorado, and recorded what I called episode one.

We were there because [Jason Amato](https://jasongamato.com/) had put a workshop together for home-service operators. By the end of it, seven of them had booked a demo.

The next morning we hiked Mt Bierstadt. 14,065 feet. That night Dennis and I talked at Denver International Airport until my 7:20 flight.
