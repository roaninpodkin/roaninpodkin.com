My older brother Colby has been in aquaculture for a long time, and he runs his own fish farm. I spent summers working with him.

It was the first business I ever saw up close. It's where I learned what a real work ethic looks like, and where I started getting ideas of my own.

One of them was about the fish. Young fish die early at a high rate, and you can see it coming in how they behave. I started thinking about software that could watch for the early signs: disease, stress, hunger, low oxygen. I still keep tabs on that idea.
