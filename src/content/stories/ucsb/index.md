I studied economics at UC Santa Barbara, class of 2026. Financial management, business strategy, marketing, entrepreneurship, managerial accounting, labor economics.

In fall 2024 I did a semester abroad in Madrid and used every weekend I could to see more of Europe. It was eye-opening.

The plan had been to join my brother after graduation. The summer before my last year, I decided to go a different direction first, and that one decision set up everything that came after.
