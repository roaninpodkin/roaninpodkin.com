The summer before my last year, I went to Asia alone for almost two months: Taiwan, Japan, Vietnam, Thailand, Singapore and Indonesia.

![Me with a backpack in front of the Liberty Square gate in Taipei](../../logbook/taipei/liberty-square.jpg)
*Taipei. First stop.*

Two things stuck. On Ko Tao, in Thailand, I got my PADI certification. In Indonesia I got on a boat in Lombok and got off four days later in Flores. In between, in Saleh Bay off Sumbawa, there were whale sharks.

Mostly, though, the trip was a chance to step back and ask: do I like who I've become, and who do I want to be? I still do that audit on purpose.
