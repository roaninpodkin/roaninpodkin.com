I came back from Asia in the middle of the AI boom and saw it as the biggest opportunity out there.

So I went deep. An hour of videos a day at minimum, most days four or five. Then testing: giving the models real tasks, seeing how accurate they were, building agents, automating the easy work.

I started telling people about it. Then I started doing it for them, and got a business license as an AI consultant. The next island picks that up.
