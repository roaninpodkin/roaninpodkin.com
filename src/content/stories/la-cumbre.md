The most notable business I worked with was La Cumbre Country Club in Santa Barbara.

On November 20, 2025, I presented to the whole team on how AI could support their work at the club. Afterwards I signed a contract to do it, and completed it.

Across different companies I built invoicing automations, knowledge-search agents and automated reporting. But the biggest part of the business turned out to be sitting down with owners one-on-one and teaching them.
